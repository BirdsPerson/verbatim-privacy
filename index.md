# Privacy Policy for Verbatim

Last updated: 2026-11-23

Verbatim is a voice-first journaling app built around a simple principle: your
journal is yours. This policy explains, plainly, what happens to your data.
The short version: your recordings, transcripts and entries never leave your
iPhone.

## On-device processing

Everything that turns your voice into a journal entry happens on your device.
Verbatim uses Apple's on-device speech recognition to transcribe what you say
and Apple's on-device Foundation Models to structure the entry. If on-device
transcription is unavailable, Verbatim does not record; it never falls back to
a server. There is no Verbatim server and no backend API. Your content is never
uploaded to us, because there is nowhere to upload it to.

## Data that stays on your device

We do not collect or transmit any of the following:

- Voice recordings. Audio is deleted after transcription unless you choose to
  keep it (Settings → Keep recordings). Kept recordings stay on your device and
  are deleted with their entry. It never leaves your phone.
- Journal entries. Your entries are stored locally on your device.
- Personally identifiable information (PII). Verbatim has no accounts, no
  sign-up, and no login. We never ask for your name, email, or any identifier.

## Analytics

Verbatim uses TelemetryDeck to understand, in aggregate, how the app is used.
It receives a small set of events (an entry was saved, by voice or by text; a
reflection question was dismissed; the upgrade screen was shown; a purchase
completed) together with general device information such as model, system
version and app version. Each event carries an anonymized identifier that
TelemetryDeck hashes so it cannot be traced back to you or your device. Events
never contain journal content, audio, transcripts, tags, or your name.

## Subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. Apple handles all billing; we never see
your payment details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription state.
RevenueCat receives your purchase history for Verbatim (which product, when,
and whether it is active) under a random anonymous ID created on your device.
It never receives your journal content, your name, or your email.

TelemetryDeck and RevenueCat act as our processors and protect this data to at
least the standard described here. We do not sell data, show ads, or track you
across other apps or websites.

## Deleting your data

Entries and recordings live only on your iPhone. Delete a single entry from
its menu, delete every kept recording in Settings → Delete all recordings, or
delete the app to remove everything. Analytics events are not tied to you, so
there is nothing to look up or delete by person. Deleting the app stops all
further collection. To ask for the anonymous purchase record held by RevenueCat
to be deleted, email us at the address below.

## Children's privacy

Verbatim does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
